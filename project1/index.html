About Me

Introduction

My name is Matthew Kim, and I am a senior at Jones College Prep in Chicago. I am part of the Class of 2027. I am Korean American, and I speak English and Korean. My family lives in South Korea, so visiting them is an important part of my summers.

During the school year, I spend my time on classes, sports, research, and school clubs. Outside of school, I help teach students at St. Paul Chong Hasang Korean School and referee youth soccer games.

Academic Interests

I am interested in mathematics, statistics, and data science. I enjoy working through a problem until I understand how it works. Through research and summer programs, I have used Python and R to study questions about financial markets, energy use, and health.

At the University of Chicago Pathways in Data Science program, I studied how the 11 S&P 500 sectors responded to changing economic conditions. I have also worked on projects about cardiovascular disease risk and the health effects of fossil fuels and renewable energy.

Soccer and Tennis

Soccer has been part of my life since third grade. I have played club soccer with Chicago Fire Youth FC and Chicago Northwind, and I play varsity soccer at Jones. My team won the Chicago Public League freshman and sophomore city championship in 2023.

I also play varsity tennis at Jones. Both sports give me time to spend with teammates and work toward a shared goal. As a U.S. Soccer Federation referee, I work with younger players and help keep their games fair.

School and Community

I serve as vice president of my school's Future Business Leaders of America chapter and Financial Literacy & Investment Club. I help organize competition preparation and work with other students on chapter activities.

At Korean school, I am a head teaching assistant. I help students learn the Korean alphabet, grammar, and culture. I also work with other teaching assistants to adapt lessons for students who need more individual support.

Visiting Korea

When I visit Korea, I stay mostly with my maternal grandparents near Siheung Sageori and visit my paternal family in Gangnam. I enjoy seeing family and friends, eating Korean food, and exploring Seoul. I have also traveled with my grandparents to places such as Jeju.

You can read more about my trips and see my photos on korea.html. My education, research, activities, and awards are listed on resume.html.
